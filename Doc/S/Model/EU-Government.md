Ti=EU Institutional and Government Model Contracts

Note=The European Commission and EU agencies publish mandatory and model contract forms for grants, public procurement, and research consortia. These are among the most thoroughly documented contract frameworks in the world. DESCA is the dominant Horizon Europe consortium agreement. The EC general conditions apply to all EU contracts.

=EU Research Grants

EC-HorizonEurope-MGA=<a href="https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/docs/2021-2027/common/agr-contr/general-mga_horizon-euratom_en.pdf">https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/docs/2021-2027/common/agr-contr/general-mga_horizon-euratom_en.pdf</a> /// Horizon Europe Model Grant Agreement (MGA). Mandatory template for all Horizon Europe grants. Version 1.2 (June 2026).

EC-AnnotatedGrantAgreement=<a href="https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/docs/2021-2027/common/guidance/aga_en.pdf">https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/docs/2021-2027/common/guidance/aga_en.pdf</a> /// Annotated Grant Agreement (AGA). EC guide explaining each clause of the MGA. Authoritative interpretation.

DESCA=<a href="https://www.desca-agreement.eu/">https://www.desca-agreement.eu/</a> /// DESCA Model Consortium Agreement for Horizon Europe. The most widely used consortium agreement template for EU research projects. Developed by Fraunhofer, Helmholtz, ANRT, EARTO, KoWi, LERU, VTT, ZENIT. Version 2.1 is current. Free to download and adapt.

DESCA-AP=<a href="https://www.desca-agreement.eu/desca-model-consortium-agreement/">https://www.desca-agreement.eu/desca-model-consortium-agreement/</a> /// DESCA Associated Partners version. For projects where external parties play a significant role.

=EU Public Procurement

EC-GeneralConditions=<a href="https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/public-procurement/legal-rules-and-implementation_en">https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/public-procurement/legal-rules-and-implementation_en</a> /// EU public procurement law and standard contract conditions. Applies to all EU-funded procurement.

EC-PRAG=<a href="https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/funding/how-to-apply/contracts_en">https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/funding/how-to-apply/contracts_en</a> /// EC Practical Guide (PRAG). Standard contracts for EU external action grants and procurement. Includes services, works, and supply contracts.

=UK Government (Post-Brexit)

Crown-Commercial=<a href="https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements">https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements</a> /// Crown Commercial Service. UK government framework agreements and call-off contracts. Successor to EU procurement frameworks.

=US Federal

FAR=<a href="https://www.acquisition.gov/far/">https://www.acquisition.gov/far/</a> /// Federal Acquisition Regulation. US federal government standard contract clauses and forms for all federal procurement.

=International Development Banks

WorldBank-ProcurementDocs=<a href="https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/products-and-services/brief/procurement-new-framework">https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/products-and-services/brief/procurement-new-framework</a> /// World Bank Standard Procurement Documents. Used in World Bank-financed projects worldwide. Includes standard bidding documents for works, goods, and services. FIDIC-based for works.
