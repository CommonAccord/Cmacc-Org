Ti=Open Contract Initiatives

Note=A movement to standardize and open-source commercial contracts has grown substantially since 2021. Bonterms, CommonPaper, and OneNDA lead this effort. These projects share the philosophy that standard contracts reduce legal friction and should be free. Most use Creative Commons licensing. See G/ for CommonAccord codifications of several of these.

=NDA

OneNDA=<a href="https://www.onenda.org/">https://www.onenda.org/</a> /// One-page global NDA standard. No modification permitted by design — either parties use it as-is or they don't. Free. Used in 100+ countries.

Bonterms-MutualNDA=<a href="https://bonterms.com/forms/mutual-nda/">https://bonterms.com/forms/mutual-nda/</a> /// Bonterms Mutual NDA. Modifiable with a variables cover sheet. CC BY 4.0.

CommonPaper-NDA=<a href="https://commonpaper.com/standards/nda/">https://commonpaper.com/standards/nda/</a> /// CommonPaper Mutual NDA. CC BY 4.0.

=SaaS / Cloud

Bonterms-CloudTerms=<a href="https://bonterms.com/forms/cloud-terms/">https://bonterms.com/forms/cloud-terms/</a> /// Bonterms Cloud Terms for SaaS/cloud services. CC BY 4.0.

CommonPaper-CSA=<a href="https://commonpaper.com/standards/cloud-service-agreement/">https://commonpaper.com/standards/cloud-service-agreement/</a> /// CommonPaper Cloud Service Agreement. CC BY 4.0.

Bonterms-SLA=<a href="https://bonterms.com/forms/service-level-agreement/">https://bonterms.com/forms/service-level-agreement/</a> /// Bonterms Service Level Agreement. CC BY 4.0.

=Data / Privacy

Bonterms-DPA=<a href="https://bonterms.com/forms/data-processing-agreement/">https://bonterms.com/forms/data-processing-agreement/</a> /// Bonterms Data Processing Agreement (GDPR/CCPA). CC BY 4.0.

CommonPaper-DPA=<a href="https://commonpaper.com/standards/data-processing-addendum/">https://commonpaper.com/standards/data-processing-addendum/</a> /// CommonPaper Data Processing Addendum. CC BY 4.0.

Bonterms-BAA=<a href="https://github.com/Bonterms/Business-Associate-Agreement">https://github.com/Bonterms/Business-Associate-Agreement</a> /// Bonterms HIPAA Business Associate Agreement. CC BY 4.0.

=Employment / IP

CommonPaper-PIIAgreement=<a href="https://commonpaper.com/standards/">https://commonpaper.com/standards/</a> /// CommonPaper suite including Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreement (PIIA). CC BY 4.0.

GitHub-BEIPA=<a href="https://github.com/github/balanced-employee-ip-agreement">https://github.com/github/balanced-employee-ip-agreement</a> /// GitHub Balanced Employee IP Agreement. CC0.

=Open Source License Contracts

Bonterms=<a href="https://bonterms.com/">https://bonterms.com/</a> /// Bonterms home. Full suite of enterprise standard agreements, all CC BY 4.0.

CommonPaper=<a href="https://commonpaper.com/standards/">https://commonpaper.com/standards/</a> /// CommonPaper home. Full suite of standards. CC BY 4.0. 40,000+ downloads.

Polyform=<a href="https://polyformproject.org/">https://polyformproject.org/</a> /// Polyform Project. Family of source-available software licenses (Noncommercial, Small Business, Perimeter, Shield, etc.). Simple, standardized alternatives to custom EULA.
