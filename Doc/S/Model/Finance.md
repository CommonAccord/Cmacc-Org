Ti=Financial Markets Model Contracts

Note=Financial markets model contracts are the most technically mature in the world — ISDA, LMA, and ICMA have produced contracts used in trillions of dollars of transactions. They are the gold standard of codification in contract practice, though their complexity and proprietary nature limit open access.

=Derivatives

ISDA=<a href="https://www.isda.org/book/isda-clause-library-isda-master-agreement/">https://www.isda.org/book/isda-clause-library-isda-master-agreement/</a> /// International Swaps and Derivatives Association Master Agreement. The global standard for OTC derivatives. ISDA Clause Library allows construction of standard-form negotiated provisions. Used in 1992 and 2002 versions under English, NY, French, and Irish law.

ISDA-Main=<a href="https://www.isda.org/">https://www.isda.org/</a> /// ISDA home. Full library of master agreements, definitions, and credit support documents.

=Loans / Syndicated Credit

LMA=<a href="https://www.lma.eu.com/resources-publications/recommended-forms">https://www.lma.eu.com/resources-publications/recommended-forms</a> /// Loan Market Association. UK/European standard for syndicated loans. Investment-grade, leveraged, real estate, and emerging market facility agreements. Developed with BBA, ACT, and major City law firms.

APLMA=<a href="https://aplma.com/">https://aplma.com/</a> /// Asia Pacific Loan Market Association. Asia-Pacific equivalent of the LMA.

LSTA=<a href="https://www.lsta.org/content/model-credit-agreement-provisions/">https://www.lsta.org/content/model-credit-agreement-provisions/</a> /// Loan Syndications and Trading Association. US syndicated loan market standard documents. Model Credit Agreement Provisions (MCAP).

=Capital Markets / Bonds

ICMA=<a href="https://www.icmagroup.org/Regulatory-Policy-and-Market-Practice/Primary-Markets/primary-market-products/private-placements/model-transaction-documentation/">https://www.icmagroup.org/Regulatory-Policy-and-Market-Practice/Primary-Markets/primary-market-products/private-placements/model-transaction-documentation/</a> /// International Capital Market Association. Model documentation for Euro private placements, subscription agreements, green and sustainability bond frameworks.

ICMA-SustainableBonds=<a href="https://www.icmagroup.org/sustainable-finance/the-principles-guidelines-and-handbooks/">https://www.icmagroup.org/sustainable-finance/the-principles-guidelines-and-handbooks/</a> /// ICMA Green Bond Principles, Social Bond Principles, Sustainability Bond Guidelines. De facto global standards for sustainable finance.

=Repo / Securities Finance

ICMA-GMRA=<a href="https://www.icmagroup.org/market-practice-and-regulatory-policy/repo-and-collateral-markets/icma-ercc-publications/icma-ercc-guides/">https://www.icmagroup.org/market-practice-and-regulatory-policy/repo-and-collateral-markets/icma-ercc-publications/icma-ercc-guides/</a> /// Global Master Repurchase Agreement (GMRA). International standard for repo transactions.

=Mortgages / Consumer

FannieMae=<a href="https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/originating-underwriting/mortgage-products/uniform-instruments">https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/originating-underwriting/mortgage-products/uniform-instruments</a> /// Fannie Mae uniform mortgage instruments (notes, deeds of trust) for all US states. Used in virtually all US residential mortgage origination.
