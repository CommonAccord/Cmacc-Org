Ti=Model Contract Documents and Clauses Hosted on GitHub

Note=GitHub has become a significant distribution channel for open contract standards. The repos below host actual contract text (not just tools). Organized by document type. Where a G/ codification exists it is noted. The awesome-legal list is the best single index of what's available.

=Aggregators and Index Lists

awesome-legal=<a href="https://github.com/ankane/awesome-legal">https://github.com/ankane/awesome-legal</a> /// Curated list of free legal documents for companies. Covers site policies, customer agreements, employee agreements, investor agreements, NDAs, and libraries. CC0. 938 stars.

open-agreements=<a href="https://github.com/open-agreements/open-agreements">https://github.com/open-agreements/open-agreements</a> /// Fills standard legal agreement templates (Bonterms, CommonPaper, YC SAFE, NVCA) and produces signable DOCX files. 25 templates. CC BY 4.0 and CC BY-ND 4.0.

Open-Source-Legal=<a href="https://github.com/Open-Source-Legal">https://github.com/Open-Source-Legal</a> /// Open Source Legal organization. Structured legal knowledge base with version control, annotation, and search.

ErichDylus-OSLE=<a href="https://github.com/ErichDylus/Open-Source-Legal-Engineering">https://github.com/ErichDylus/Open-Source-Legal-Engineering</a> /// Open source legal templates for crypto/DAO/web3 — formation docs, NDA, DAO contractor agreements, solidity smart contract wrappers.

Startup-Starter-Pack=<a href="https://github.com/Ro5s/Startup-Starter-Pack">https://github.com/Ro5s/Startup-Starter-Pack</a> /// Collection of model legal documents for startup setup and fundraising: SAFE, Series Seed, Series AA, NDA, SaaS agreement.
