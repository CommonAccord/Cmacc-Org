Ti=Data Sharing and Privacy Model Agreements

Note=Data sharing agreements are increasingly standardized across biomedical research, corporate GDPR compliance, open data, and identity management. The EU Standard Contractual Clauses are legally mandated for EU data exports; GA4GH sets standards for genomic research; DESCA governs EU research consortia.

=EU Regulatory Standards

EU-StandardContractualClauses=<a href="https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/standard-contractual-clauses-scc_en">https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/standard-contractual-clauses-scc_en</a> /// EU Standard Contractual Clauses for international data transfers. Legally required for GDPR-compliant transfers to non-adequate third countries. Controller-to-controller and controller-to-processor versions.

EDPB=<a href="https://edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/documents/public-consultations/2022/recommendations-012022-guidelines-articles-46_en">https://edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/documents/public-consultations/2022/recommendations-012022-guidelines-articles-46_en</a> /// European Data Protection Board guidelines on Article 46 transfer mechanisms (SCCs, BCRs, etc.).

=Biomedical / Research

GA4GH=<a href="https://www.ga4gh.org/">https://www.ga4gh.org/</a> /// Global Alliance for Genomics and Health. Framework for responsible sharing of genomic and health-related data. Standards, policies, and model agreements for data access, consent, and ethics review. Free and open-source.

GA4GH-Framework=<a href="https://www.ga4gh.org/product/framework-for-responsible-sharing-of-genomic-and-health-related-data/">https://www.ga4gh.org/product/framework-for-responsible-sharing-of-genomic-and-health-related-data/</a> /// GA4GH Framework for Responsible Sharing of Genomic and Health-Related Data.

NIH-DataManagement=<a href="https://sharing.nih.gov/data-management-and-sharing-policy">https://sharing.nih.gov/data-management-and-sharing-policy</a> /// NIH Data Management and Sharing Policy. Mandatory for NIH-funded research. Includes model data management plans.

OECD-Health-Data=<a href="https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/health-data-governance.html">https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/health-data-governance.html</a> /// OECD recommendations on health data governance and sharing.

=Open Data

CDLA=<a href="https://cdla.dev/">https://cdla.dev/</a> /// Community Data License Agreement (Linux Foundation). Permissive and Sharing variants for open data sets.

Microsoft-OpenUseOfData=<a href="https://github.com/microsoft/Open-Use-of-Data-Agreement">https://github.com/microsoft/Open-Use-of-Data-Agreement</a> /// Microsoft Open Use of Data Agreement (O-UDA). Open license for datasets.

=Identity / Personal Data

Kantara-UMA=<a href="https://kantarainitiative.org/confluence/display/uma/UMA+Legal">https://kantarainitiative.org/confluence/display/uma/UMA+Legal</a> /// Kantara Initiative User Managed Access (UMA) Legal. Framework for user-controlled data sharing.

MyData=<a href="https://mydata.org/declaration/">https://mydata.org/declaration/</a> /// MyData Global Declaration. Principles for human-centric personal data management.

=Research Consortium

DESCA=<a href="https://www.desca-agreement.eu/">https://www.desca-agreement.eu/</a> /// DESCA Model Consortium Agreement for EU research projects (Horizon Europe). Mandatory consortium agreement template. Maintained by Fraunhofer, Helmholtz, and major European research organizations. Most widely used Horizon Europe consortium agreement.

EUDAT=<a href="https://www.eudat.eu/">https://www.eudat.eu/</a> /// EUDAT collaborative data infrastructure for European research. Common data access agreements and interoperability standards.

=Financial Data

FIBO=<a href="https://spec.edmcouncil.org/fibo/">https://spec.edmcouncil.org/fibo/</a> /// Financial Industry Business Ontology. EDM Council standard for financial data semantics — provides common vocabulary for financial contracts and instruments.
