Ti=Construction and Engineering Model Contracts

Note=Construction contracts are among the most mature areas of model contract development. FIDIC is the dominant international standard; NEC and JCT lead in the UK; AIA leads in the US. BIMCO covers maritime/offshore. These are sophisticated, deeply negotiated standards developed over decades.

=International

FIDIC=<a href="https://fidic.org/themes/fidic-contracts">https://fidic.org/themes/fidic-contracts</a> /// International Federation of Consulting Engineers. The global standard for major infrastructure and construction projects. Suite includes Red Book (construction), Yellow Book (design-build), Silver Book (EPC turnkey), White Book (services), Gold Book (design-build-operate). Used by World Bank, regional development banks, and governments worldwide.

FIDIC-WhichContract=<a href="https://fidic.org/bookshop/about-bookshop/which-fidic-contract-should-i-use">https://fidic.org/bookshop/about-bookshop/which-fidic-contract-should-i-use</a> /// FIDIC guide to selecting the right contract form.

ICC-Construction=<a href="https://iccwbo.org/business-solutions/model-contracts-clauses/">https://iccwbo.org/business-solutions/model-contracts-clauses/</a> /// ICC model construction-related contracts and clauses.


=UK

NEC=<a href="https://www.neccontract.com/products/contracts">https://www.neccontract.com/products/contracts</a> /// New Engineering Contract (NEC). Institution of Civil Engineers. Widely used for UK public procurement. Collaborative, process-oriented approach. NEC4 is the current edition.

JCT=<a href="https://www.jctltd.co.uk/">https://www.jctltd.co.uk/</a> /// Joint Contracts Tribunal. UK standard building and construction contracts. Suite includes Standard Building Contract, Design and Build, Minor Works. JCT 2024 is the current edition.

=US

AIA=<a href="https://aiacontracts.com/">https://aiacontracts.com/</a> /// American Institute of Architects. Nearly 200 standard forms for design, construction, and related services. Over 125 years of development. A-Series (owner-contractor), B-Series (owner-architect).

=Maritime / Offshore

BIMCO=<a href="https://www.bimco.org/contractual-affairs/bimco-contracts/">https://www.bimco.org/contractual-affairs/bimco-contracts/</a> /// Baltic and International Maritime Council. Over 350 standard contracts and clauses covering the full lifecycle of a vessel: charter parties, ship management, sale and purchase, crew, security. GENCON, BARECON, SHIPMAN are industry standards.
