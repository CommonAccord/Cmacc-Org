Ti=Climate, ESG, and Sustainability Contract Clauses

Note=A growing body of model clauses embeds climate and sustainability obligations in commercial contracts. The Chancery Lane Project is the most developed open source initiative. ICMA's Green Bond Principles dominate sustainable finance. The EU CSDDD creates binding supply chain due diligence obligations.

=Model Clause Libraries

ChanceryLaneProject=<a href="https://chancerylaneproject.org/clauses/">https://chancerylaneproject.org/clauses/</a> /// The Chancery Lane Project. 120+ free climate clauses for insertion in commercial contracts. Covers procurement, employment, finance, real estate, dispute resolution. Three editions of the Climate Contract Playbook. CC BY 4.0.

ChanceryLane-Playbook=<a href="https://chancerylaneproject.org/climate-contract-playbook/">https://chancerylaneproject.org/climate-contract-playbook/</a> /// Chancery Lane Climate Contract Playbook. Full compilation of TCLP model clauses with guidance notes.

WorldCC-Sustainability=<a href="https://www.worldcc.com/Resources/Tools">https://www.worldcc.com/Resources/Tools</a> /// WorldCC sustainability contracting resources. Model language for embedding ESG requirements in commercial agreements.

=Green Finance Standards

ICMA-GreenBondPrinciples=<a href="https://www.icmagroup.org/sustainable-finance/the-principles-guidelines-and-handbooks/green-bond-principles-gbp/">https://www.icmagroup.org/sustainable-finance/the-principles-guidelines-and-handbooks/green-bond-principles-gbp/</a> /// ICMA Green Bond Principles. De facto global standard for green bond issuance and disclosure. Voluntary framework.

ICMA-SocialBond=<a href="https://www.icmagroup.org/sustainable-finance/the-principles-guidelines-and-handbooks/social-bond-principles-sbp/">https://www.icmagroup.org/sustainable-finance/the-principles-guidelines-and-handbooks/social-bond-principles-sbp/</a> /// ICMA Social Bond Principles.

ICMA-SustainabilityLinked=<a href="https://www.icmagroup.org/sustainable-finance/the-principles-guidelines-and-handbooks/sustainability-linked-bond-principles-slbp/">https://www.icmagroup.org/sustainable-finance/the-principles-guidelines-and-handbooks/sustainability-linked-bond-principles-slbp/</a> /// ICMA Sustainability-Linked Bond Principles.

LMA-SustainabilityLinkedLoans=<a href="https://www.lma.eu.com/sustainable-lending">https://www.lma.eu.com/sustainable-lending</a> /// LMA Sustainability Linked Loan Principles and Green Loan Principles.

=Regulatory Frameworks

EU-CSDDD=<a href="https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024L1760">https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024L1760</a> /// EU Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive. Binding supply chain due diligence and climate obligations for large EU companies and their suppliers.

EU-SFDR=<a href="https://www.esma.europa.eu/databases-library/esma-library/sustainable-finance">https://www.esma.europa.eu/databases-library/esma-library/sustainable-finance</a> /// EU Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation. Mandatory ESG disclosure for financial market participants.

TCFD=<a href="https://www.fsb-tcfd.org/">https://www.fsb-tcfd.org/</a> /// Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures. Model framework for climate-related disclosures in contracts and reporting.

=Supply Chain / Procurement

Sedex=<a href="https://www.sedex.com/">https://www.sedex.com/</a> /// Sedex. Responsible supply chain platform with model ethical trade requirements.

EcoVadis=<a href="https://ecovadis.com/">https://ecovadis.com/</a> /// EcoVadis. Sustainability ratings platform whose requirements are increasingly embedded in supplier contracts.
