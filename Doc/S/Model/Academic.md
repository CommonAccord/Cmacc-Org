Ti=Academic Model Contract Law Projects

Note=European legal academics have produced major soft-law codifications of contract law — PECL, DCFR, and the UNIDROIT PICC. These are not contract forms but model rules intended to influence national law, guide arbitrators, and provide a common framework for international contracts. They represent the most rigorous academic work on contract law harmonization.

=European Principles and Model Rules

PECL=<a href="https://www.trans-lex.org/400200/_/pecl/">https://www.trans-lex.org/400200/_/pecl/</a> /// Principles of European Contract Law (PECL). Lando Commission work, 1995-2003. Three parts covering general contract law. Foundation for the DCFR.

DCFR=<a href="https://www.law.kuleuven.be/personal/mstorme/DCFR_outline.pdf">https://www.law.kuleuven.be/personal/mstorme/DCFR_outline.pdf</a> /// Draft Common Frame of Reference. Study Group on a European Civil Code + Research Group on EU Private Law. 2009. The most comprehensive attempt at European private law codification. Covers contracts, property, unjust enrichment, and non-contractual liability.

ELI=<a href="https://www.europeanlawinstitute.eu/projects-publications/completed-projects-and-publications/">https://www.europeanlawinstitute.eu/projects-publications/completed-projects-and-publications/</a> /// European Law Institute. Produces model rules, statements, and guidance on European private law including contracts, insolvency, and digital assets.

CESL=<a href="https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:52011PC0635">https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:52011PC0635</a> /// Common European Sales Law. Proposed EU regulation (2011, withdrawn 2014). Opt-in contract law for cross-border B2C and SME B2B sales. Influenced by DCFR.

=International Principles

UNIDROIT-PICC=<a href="https://www.unidroit.org/contracts/">https://www.unidroit.org/contracts/</a> /// UNIDROIT Principles of International Commercial Contracts (PICC). 2016 edition. 211 articles covering the full lifecycle of international commercial contracts. Used in arbitration as gap-fillers and as governing law. With model clauses for incorporation.

ALI-UNIDROIT-Procedure=<a href="https://www.unidroit.org/instruments/transnational-civil-procedure/">https://www.unidroit.org/instruments/transnational-civil-procedure/</a> /// ALI/UNIDROIT Principles of Transnational Civil Procedure. Model rules for international commercial litigation (as opposed to arbitration). English, French, Spanish versions.

CISG=<a href="https://uncitral.un.org/en/texts/salegoods/conventions/sale_of_goods/cisg">https://uncitral.un.org/en/texts/salegoods/conventions/sale_of_goods/cisg</a> /// UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods. In force 97 countries. Applies automatically to cross-border goods contracts unless excluded.

=Research Tools and Databases

Trans-Lex=<a href="https://www.trans-lex.org/">https://www.trans-lex.org/</a> /// Trans-Lex.org. Database of transnational commercial law texts — PICC, PECL, DCFR, Incoterms, CISG, and other model rules. Free, comprehensive, linked.

CLOUT=<a href="https://uncitral.un.org/en/case_law">https://uncitral.un.org/en/case_law</a> /// UNCITRAL Case Law on Uniform Texts (CLOUT). Database of court decisions and arbitral awards on UNCITRAL texts including CISG and model laws.
