Ti=General Commercial and Trade Model Contracts

Note=These organizations have produced model contracts for general international commercial use — sale of goods, agency, distribution, services. The ICC and UNIDROIT are the dominant international standard-setters. Several of these are partially codified in G/.

=International / Cross-Border

ICC-ModelContracts=<a href="https://iccwbo.org/business-solutions/model-contracts-clauses/">https://iccwbo.org/business-solutions/model-contracts-clauses/</a> /// International Chamber of Commerce. Covers agency, distribution, sale, franchise, IP licensing, joint ventures, start-up agreements. The most widely used international commercial model contract suite.

UNIDROIT-PICC=<a href="https://www.unidroit.org/contracts/">https://www.unidroit.org/contracts/</a> /// UNIDROIT Principles of International Commercial Contracts (PICC). Soft law principles for international contracts, with model clauses for incorporation. 211 articles in 11 chapters.

CISG=<a href="https://uncitral.un.org/en/texts/salegoods/conventions/sale_of_goods/cisg">https://uncitral.un.org/en/texts/salegoods/conventions/sale_of_goods/cisg</a> /// UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods. In force in 97 countries. The background law for most international commercial contracts.

UNCITRAL-ModelLaw=<a href="https://uncitral.un.org/en/texts/">https://uncitral.un.org/en/texts/</a> /// UNCITRAL model laws and texts on contracts, electronic commerce, and transferable records.

=European

ORGALIME=<a href="https://orgalim.eu/en/legal-publications/?ZcWfMD_category=76">https://orgalim.eu/en/legal-publications/?ZcWfMD_category=76</a> /// European engineering industries model contracts. General conditions for supply and erection of mechanical, electrical, and electronic products. Seems a paying model.

=NDA / Confidentiality

OneNDA=<a href="https://www.onenda.org/">https://www.onenda.org/</a> /// Global standard one-page NDA. Take-it-or-leave-it — no modification permitted by design. Free.

OneNDA.=[G/OneNDA-NDA/X/Original/2022-v7-2-Demo-Acme-Ang.md] /// OneNDA standard form. Multiple solutions.

Bonterms-NDA=<a href="https://bonterms.com/forms/mutual-nda/">https://bonterms.com/forms/mutual-nda/</a> /// Bonterms Mutual NDA. CC BY 4.0. Companion to Bonterms Cloud Terms.

CommonPaper=<a href="https://commonpaper.com/standards/">https://commonpaper.com/standards/</a> /// Common Paper standards for commercial contracts, including Mutual NDA, Cloud Service Agreement, and more. CC BY 4.0.

Bonterms-CloudTerms=<a href="https://bonterms.com/forms/cloud-terms/">https://bonterms.com/forms/cloud-terms/</a> /// Bonterms Cloud Terms: SaaS/cloud service standard agreement. CC BY 4.0.
