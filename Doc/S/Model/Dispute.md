Ti=Dispute Resolution Rules and Model Clauses

Note=Arbitration institutions publish model clauses for inclusion in contracts and full procedural rules. The ICC, LCIA, SCC, AAA, and JAMS are the dominant institutions. Several are partially codified in G/. Each institution also publishes recommended arbitration clauses that parties can embed in their contracts.

=International Arbitration Institutions

ICC=<a href="https://iccwbo.org/dispute-resolution/dispute-resolution-services/arbitration/rules-of-arbitration/">https://iccwbo.org/dispute-resolution/dispute-resolution-services/arbitration/rules-of-arbitration/</a> /// International Chamber of Commerce. ICC Arbitration Rules. Recommended clause for inclusion in contracts. The most widely used international arbitration institution.

ICC-ModelClause=<a href="https://iccwbo.org/dispute-resolution/dispute-resolution-services/arbitration/icc-arbitration-clause/">https://iccwbo.org/dispute-resolution/dispute-resolution-services/arbitration/icc-arbitration-clause/</a> /// ICC recommended arbitration clause.

LCIA=<a href="https://www.lcia.org/dispute_resolution_services/lcia_recommended_clauses.aspx">https://www.lcia.org/dispute_resolution_services/lcia_recommended_clauses.aspx</a> /// London Court of International Arbitration. Recommended clauses for LCIA arbitration, LCIA mediation, and LCIA/UNCITRAL proceedings.

SCC=<a href="https://sccinstitute.com/our-services/model-clauses/">https://sccinstitute.com/our-services/model-clauses/</a> /// Stockholm Chamber of Commerce Arbitration Institute. Model clauses for arbitration and expedited arbitration.

SIAC=<a href="https://www.siac.org.sg/model-clauses/">https://www.siac.org.sg/model-clauses/</a> /// Singapore International Arbitration Centre. Model clauses for SIAC arbitration.

VIAC=<a href="https://www.viac.eu/en/arbitration/arbitration-clause">https://www.viac.eu/en/arbitration/arbitration-clause</a> /// Vienna International Arbitral Centre. Model arbitration clause.

=US Arbitration Institutions

AAA=<a href="https://www.adr.org/ClauseCenter">https://www.adr.org/ClauseCenter</a> /// American Arbitration Association. Model arbitration clauses for commercial, consumer, employment, and construction disputes.

JAMS=<a href="https://www.jamsadr.com/clauses/">https://www.jamsadr.com/clauses/</a> /// JAMS. Comprehensive arbitration clause library with optional provisions for confidentiality, law, expedited procedures, and appeal.

=UNCITRAL Model Rules

UNCITRAL-Arbitration=<a href="https://uncitral.un.org/en/texts/arbitration/contractualtexts/arbitration">https://uncitral.un.org/en/texts/arbitration/contractualtexts/arbitration</a> /// UNCITRAL Arbitration Rules. Widely used for ad hoc arbitration, including in investor-state disputes and PCA proceedings.

UNCITRAL-Mediation=<a href="https://uncitral.un.org/en/texts/mediation/conventions/international_settlement_agreements">https://uncitral.un.org/en/texts/mediation/conventions/international_settlement_agreements</a> /// Singapore Convention on Mediation. UNCITRAL model mediation clause and convention.

=Academic / International Law

ALI-UNIDROIT=<a href="https://www.unidroit.org/instruments/transnational-civil-procedure/">https://www.unidroit.org/instruments/transnational-civil-procedure/</a> /// ALI/UNIDROIT Principles of Transnational Civil Procedure. Academic model rules for international litigation. Multilingual.

=Specialized

WIPO-Arbitration=<a href="https://www.wipo.int/amc/en/arbitration/clauses/">https://www.wipo.int/amc/en/arbitration/clauses/</a> /// WIPO Arbitration and Mediation Center. Model clauses for IP and technology disputes.

Groton=<a href="https://jimgroton.com/">https://jimgroton.com/</a> /// Jim Groton — dispute prevention and standing neutral frameworks. "The Father of the Modern Standing Neutral Concept." De-escalation protocols for commercial and construction disputes.
