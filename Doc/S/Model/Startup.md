Ti=Startup and Venture Capital Model Documents

Note=Startup financing documents have been extensively standardized, primarily in the US. NVCA is the most comprehensive; Y Combinator's SAFE is the most widely used seed instrument. The Galion Project and Bondard cover French law. Multiple sources have codified the same documents from different angles — see G/ for the CommonAccord codifications.

=US — Seed / Pre-Seed

YCombinator-SAFE=<a href="https://www.ycombinator.com/documents">https://www.ycombinator.com/documents</a> /// Y Combinator's Simple Agreement for Future Equity (SAFE). The dominant US seed financing instrument. Post-money SAFE is the current standard (2018+). Also includes pro rata side letter, SAFE primer.

SeriesSeed=<a href="https://www.seriesseed.com/">https://www.seriesseed.com/</a> /// Series Seed Documents. Open source seed equity financing documents created by Fenwick & West. Minimal, founder-friendly alternative to NVCA for seed rounds.

SeriesSeed-GitHub=<a href="https://github.com/seriesseed/equity">https://github.com/seriesseed/equity</a> /// Series Seed on GitHub. Community-maintained and updatable.

500Startups-KISS=<a href="https://www.cooleygo.com/documents/kiss-convertible-debt-equity-agreements/">https://www.cooleygo.com/documents/kiss-convertible-debt-equity-agreements/</a> /// Keep It Simple Security (KISS). 500 Startups convertible debt/equity instrument for seed rounds. Available via CooleyGo.

Techstars-Bridge=<a href="https://www.techstars.com/the-line/advice/techstars-model-bridge-loan-financing-documents">https://www.techstars.com/the-line/advice/techstars-model-bridge-loan-financing-documents</a> /// Techstars Bridge Financing Documents. Model bridge note for accelerator-backed companies.

=US — Series A / Equity

NVCA=<a href="https://nvca.org/model-legal-documents/">https://nvca.org/model-legal-documents/</a> /// National Venture Capital Association model legal documents. The US industry standard for Series A+ equity financings. Includes term sheet, stock purchase agreement, investors' rights agreement, voting agreement, ROFR/co-sale, COI, management rights, indemnification, and LP agreement.

CooleyGo-NVCA=<a href="https://www.cooleygo.com/documents/nvca-financing-documents/">https://www.cooleygo.com/documents/nvca-financing-documents/</a> /// CooleyGo's NVCA document generator. Interactive tool for generating customized NVCA documents.

YCombinator-SeriesAA=<a href="https://old.ycombinator.com/seriesaa/">https://old.ycombinator.com/seriesaa/</a> /// Y Combinator Series AA equity financing documents. Simplified series A alternative.

=US — Employee Equity

GitHub-BEIPA=<a href="https://github.com/github/balanced-employee-ip-agreement">https://github.com/github/balanced-employee-ip-agreement</a> /// GitHub's Balanced Employee IP Agreement. Employee-friendly IP assignment that carves out personal projects. CC0.

=Europe — France

GalionProject=<a href="https://www.thegalionproject.com/en/outils">https://www.thegalionproject.com/en/outils</a> /// The Galion Project. French VC term sheet, BSPCE kit, board guide, fundraiser guidelines. Open source standard for French tech startups. Term sheet has 20,000+ downloads.

GalionProject-TermSheet=<a href="https://www.thegalionproject.com/en/outils/galion-term-sheet">https://www.thegalionproject.com/en/outils/galion-term-sheet</a> /// Galion Term Sheet (Series A). The French standard for VC term sheets.

GalionProject-BSPCE=<a href="https://www.thegalionproject.com/en/outils/kit-bspce">https://www.thegalionproject.com/en/outils/kit-bspce</a> /// Galion BSPCE Kit. French employee warrants (bons de souscription de parts de créateur d'entreprise).

=Europe — UK

SeedLegals=<a href="https://seedlegals.com/">https://seedlegals.com/</a> /// SeedLegals. UK-focused automated startup legal documents. Based on standardized templates for SEIS/EIS rounds, term sheets, employment contracts.

=International

SAFT=<a href="https://saftproject.com/">https://saftproject.com/</a> /// Simple Agreement for Future Tokens. Token sale instrument developed by Protocol Labs and Cooley for blockchain projects.
