Ti=Index of the CommonAccord library (G)

Note=This index organises the materials in G by subject, in the manner of the policy areas of the European Commission: data, digital and AI; climate and responsible business; health and research; the single market; intellectual property; finance and company law; work; and justice. Inside each chapter the flow is the same: binding law first, then principles and official guidance, then model agreements, then tools. A document that belongs in two places is listed once in full and cross-listed elsewhere as See also. Each entry shows its jurisdiction and, where not English, its language.  Key conventions: clicking a key ending with a dot (.) shows the rendered document; a key ending with a hyphen (-) shows the target in source view; a key ending with a slash (/) lists the target folder.

=<b>Chapters</b>

Data-=[Index/Data/1ndex.md] /// <b>Data, Digital Rights & Privacy</b>. Personal-data protection, data sharing and data spaces, consent and digital identity, and the governance of the internet and online platforms. Read this chapter alongside the GDPR, the Data Governance Act, the Data Act and the common European data spaces: it holds the legal texts, regulator guidance, and the model agreements that put them into practice. (60 documents)

AI-=[Index/AI/1ndex.md] /// <b>Artificial Intelligence</b>. The EU AI Act and other AI law, principles and governance frameworks, definitions of open AI, and model contracts for AI development and data use. (15 documents)

Sustainability-=[Index/Sustainability/1ndex.md] /// <b>Climate, Environment, Energy & Responsible Business</b>. International climate and environmental instruments, nuclear and radiation safety, supply-chain due diligence and sustainability clauses, and energy contracts. Read this chapter alongside the European Green Deal and the Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive. (13 documents)

Health-=[Index/Health/1ndex.md] /// <b>Health, Life Sciences & Research</b>. Bioethics, research-data policy, genomic and biomedical data sharing, patient privacy, and technology transfer from universities and public research bodies. Read this chapter alongside the European Health Data Space and the Horizon programmes. (21 documents)

Commerce-=[Index/Commerce/1ndex.md] /// <b>Single Market: Commercial Contracts</b>. Contracts between businesses and with consumers and public bodies: framework and master agreements, confidentiality, cloud and online terms, sector-specific contracts, public procurement conditions, and clause libraries. Many documents here come in several languages and national variants. (67 documents)

IP-=[Index/IP/1ndex.md] /// <b>Intellectual Property, Open Licences & Open Source</b>. Copyright, patent and software licences, open hardware, defensive patent pledges, and contributor and project-governance terms. (22 documents)

Finance-=[Index/Finance/1ndex.md] /// <b>Company Law, Finance & Digital Assets</b>. Company formation and governance, venture and growth finance, mergers and acquisitions, funds, lending and insurance, and digital assets and distributed-ledger governance. (46 documents)

Work-=[Index/Work/1ndex.md] /// <b>Employment, Contributors & Professional Services</b>. Employment and contractor agreements, invention assignment, volunteer and contributor terms, and engagements with professionals. (15 documents)

Justice-=[Index/Justice/1ndex.md] /// <b>Justice, Fundamental Rights & Dispute Resolution</b>. Constitutions and charters, civil procedure and court forms, arbitration and mediation clauses, and de-escalation. Read this chapter alongside the Commission's work on access to justice and cross-border civil procedure. (17 documents)

Method-=[Index/Method/1ndex.md] /// <b>Standards, Ontologies & Method</b>. How the library works: the Prose Object model, shared building blocks, taxonomies and vocabularies, tooling experiments, and research on contract design. (24 documents)
