Much of "the law" is a set of naming conventions for shared ideas. So is the web.
  • All of the shared "ProseObjects" are in the "G" folder. G is for git, since ProseObjects should be shared and improved as git repositories.
  • Other materials are historic. Some contain very interesting experiments about organizing and naming documents. Some are collections of documents. Most of them use older naming conventions and some are quite incomplete.
  • You can cruise the materials in the G/ folder or look by subject in the Index/1ndex.md.
A place to start might be with OTF/WorldCC/Dublin-Present-CmA/Acme-Quake-FoodSupply/, our little food supply contract demo for the WorldCC.
